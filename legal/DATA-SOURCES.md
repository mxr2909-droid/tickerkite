# Data Sources

WIRE downloads all of its data directly to your Mac from the sources below. We don't own or control any of them, and none of them endorses WIRE. Data can be delayed, wrong or missing, and any source can change or stop working without notice.

## Prices and charts

- **Yahoo Finance** (finance.yahoo.com): delayed quotes, charts and symbol search. This is Yahoo's public website data, not a licensed feed.
- **Finnhub** (finnhub.io): prices for US stocks and ETFs, only if you add your own API key in Settings.

## News

- **Publisher RSS feeds**: CNBC, MarketWatch, Yahoo Finance, BBC, Nikkei Asia, Financial Times, the Federal Reserve and the ECB. Each publisher owns its headlines. WIRE shows what the publisher's public feed includes and links to the full article.
- **Google News** (news.google.com): public RSS searches that find stories from other outlets. Each story links to its original publisher.
- You can add, remove or turn off feeds under View › Edit Feeds….

## Economic data and calendars

- **FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis** (fred.stlouisfed.org): economic time series. Some series on FRED are copyrighted by their original publishers, who are named on each series' page:
  - ICE Data Indices: BAMLC0A0CM, BAMLH0A0HYM2
  - Cboe: VIXCLS
  - University of Michigan: UMCSENT
  - S&P Dow Jones Indices: CSUSHPINSA
  - Freddie Mac: MORTGAGE30US
  - OECD, Eurostat and the ECB: international series, used with attribution
- **US Treasury** (home.treasury.gov): daily yield curves.
- **Federal Reserve Board** (federalreserve.gov): FOMC calendar and projections.
- **BLS, BEA and the Census Bureau**: release schedules.
- **Nasdaq** (nasdaq.com): economic calendar forecasts and actuals, earnings dates and institutional holders.
- **SEC EDGAR** (sec.gov): company filings, financial statements and insider trades. Public domain.
