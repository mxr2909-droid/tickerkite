Tickerkite is a desktop app. It has no account system, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't run servers that receive your data. This policy explains what stays on your Mac and what Tickerkite sends elsewhere.

## What stays on your Mac

Tickerkite stores these files in ~/Library/Application Support/Tickerkite. They never leave your Mac:

- your watchlist, portfolio holdings, saved charts and settings
- news, price and economic-data caches
- your license status

If you add a Finnhub API key, Tickerkite encrypts it with your macOS Keychain before saving it.

## What Tickerkite sends, and where

- **News and market data requests.** Tickerkite downloads headlines, prices and economic data directly from each source listed under Data Sources. Like any website you visit, those sources see your IP address and that the request comes from Tickerkite. Tickerkite doesn't tell them what is on your watchlist or in your portfolio, beyond the symbol it is asking about.
- **SEC contact email.** If you open a company's filings, the SEC asks apps for a contact email. Tickerkite sends the email you enter only to sec.gov, in the request header.
- **Finnhub key.** If you add one, it goes only to finnhub.io, with the stock symbols Tickerkite asks it to quote.
- **License checks.** When you activate Tickerkite, and about twice a day after that, Tickerkite sends your license key, an activation ID and your Mac's name to Lemon Squeezy (lemonsqueezy.com) to check that the license is valid. Your Mac's name is sent so you can tell your activations apart.
- **Purchases.** Lemon Squeezy handles payment as our reseller. We receive your name, email address and order details from them, but never your card details. Their privacy policy covers what they collect.
- **Update checks.** Tickerkite checks GitHub (github.com) for new versions. GitHub sees your IP address.

## What we do with what we receive

We use your name, email address and order details from Lemon Squeezy only to provide your license, give support and send important notices about Tickerkite. We don't sell or share them, except as the law requires.

- To remove everything Tickerkite stored, delete the Tickerkite folder in ~/Library/Application Support.
